# 🚀 Creative Technologist & Full-Stack Architect

### 👨‍💻 Professional Summary
I am a results-oriented **Full-Stack Developer** and **UI/UX Designer** specialized in building high-performance, scalable systems and visually compelling digital products. With a unique blend of engineering precision and creative vision, I bridge the gap between complex backend architecture and intuitive user experiences. My expertise spans the entire development lifecycle—from conceptual design in **Adobe XD** and **Figma** to deployment on distributed cloud infrastructure like **AWS**, **GCP**, and **Vercel**.

### ⚡ Core Expertise
- **Frontend Engineering:** Crafting responsive, interactive UIs using **Next.js**, **Vue.js**, and modern JavaScript/TypeScript.
- **Backend Architecture:** Developing robust APIs and server-side logic with **Node.js**, **PHP**, and optimized **MySQL/Postgres** databases.
- **Cloud & DevOps:** Managing scalable infrastructure, automated deployments, and CDN optimizations via **AWS**, **Cloudflare**, and **Nginx**.
- **Creative Design:** Leading brand identity and UI/UX projects using the **Adobe Creative Suite** (Photoshop, Illustrator, XD).
